# Clocks: estop.log = host clock; robot_udp.log = container clock = host - 7 h.

## Pad facts
- Receiver streams ~121 raw HID reports/s on both interfaces whether or not anything is pressed (no heartbeat usable to detect a dead pad).
- Measured: switching the pad OFF while R1 (button 7) is held makes the receiver emit a RELEASE for R1 (js events: press 19.55 s, release 28.57 s when the pad was switched off). Not measured: flat batteries, out of range. A short delay between flipping the switch and the release is the pad/receiver link timeout.
- Pad sleeps when idle (user's observation); a sleeping pad cannot hold R1, so the robot cannot move (fail-safe).
