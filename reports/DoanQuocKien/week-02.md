## Decisions
- Camera stays at 2.6 deg down, no 15 deg wedge: from the fisheye geometry the wedge gains about 3 cm of nearer floor and loses the torso of a person at the 1.2 m standoff (highest visible point about 1.2 m -> 0.8 m). To confirm with a 5 min field-of-view check at 1.2 m. The screw must not be touched: a movement of 1 deg changes the distance at 2 m by about 7 %.
- **The live marker tilt is the primary tilt source; the strips value (5.03 deg) is only the start value and the end-of-session check.** The chassis tilt changes by 1.7 deg between floor spots, so a single calibrated tilt cannot be good to better than about 1 deg on this floor.
- Marker heights come from the A4 print layout (square lower edge 132 mm above the page bottom, pages stand on the floor against the wall); marker size 0.15 m.
- Usable drive speeds stay v 0.4-1.0 at cap 0.3; drive tests need start checks and short no-detection guards.
